## 1. The harness, on D-061's label and on cached distances

`analyse_bank.py` is the harness entry point (T2.4): naive-transferred, matched-per-operator and group-conditional calibration, each with coverage, miss rate, flag area and region FDR per operator, plus the gap-against-distance trend. Two changes made it usable on CT:

- **The shared label** (T2.7 §1). Its old `--label-threshold` defaulted to an absolute constant of 0.10, P-02's failure mode, so it now takes the same `--label` options as every other script, with D-061's absolute label by default. Its 8 tests were updated to match: banks without a design side read `--label ratio`, and a new test checks that the default refuses such a bank with the hint.
- **`--distances-from`.** The harness used to recompute every distance on the CPU, at minutes per CT pair. It now reads `ct_distances.py`'s output (T2.7 §6), so a CT harness run takes seconds. A pair missing from the file is refused rather than silently zero.

The CT distances from the 30-view operator (8 pairs, 12 min on the GPU) were added so the harness and the figures can run in **both** directions, as D-056 requires. `ct_distances.py --calibration` resumes into the same CSV, skipping pairs already there.

Ordered by distance, naive coverage falls almost monotonically: the gap-against-distance rank correlation is **ρ = +0.950**. Group-conditional calibration has none (ρ = +0.267, gaps −0.02 to +0.045).

**What restoring coverage costs.** At every deployment operator, group-conditional calibration flags more than naive transfer and accepts more false discoveries. At 30 views, flag area rises from 0.220 to 0.313 and region FDR from 0.029 to 0.106; at 90°, from 0.308 to 0.423 and 0.084 to 0.202. Naive transfer's near-zero FDR at the sparse operators is not precision. It flags too little, mostly in the regions that are hallucinated, and misses a quarter to a third of them. The calibration operator's FDR of 0.435 reflects the densest-view operator's low positive rate (7.6% of its regions), not a defect.

## 2a. CT, calibrated at 30 views

The signed distances are antisymmetric, as D-053's construction requires: 180 → 30 views is +0.602 and 30 → 180 views is −0.601.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sparse v30` *(calib.)* | 0.000 | 0.868 | 0.387 | 0.282 | 0.868 | 0.387 | 0.282 |
| `sparse v45` | −0.225 | 0.991 | 0.565 | 0.547 | 0.873 | 0.319 | 0.294 |
| `sparse v60` | −0.323 | 1.000 | 0.824 | 0.737 | 0.899 | 0.348 | 0.441 |
| `limited a90` | −0.427 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.606 | 0.878 | 0.759 | 0.543 |
| `sparse v90` | −0.439 | 1.000 | 0.997 | 0.821 | 0.917 | 0.512 | 0.681 |
| `limited a120` | −0.470 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.806 | 0.897 | 0.515 | 0.663 |
| `limited a150` | −0.492 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.860 | 0.905 | 0.609 | 0.792 |
| `sparse v120` | −0.513 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.866 | 0.885 | 0.603 | 0.804 |
| `sparse v180` | −0.601 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.924 | 0.897 | 0.863 | 0.921 |

- **In this direction the failure is over-flagging, and it grows with the shift.** Naive transfer holds coverage at 1.000 at 7 of 8 operators by flagging more as the deployed operator moves further away: 0.565 → 0.824 → ~1.000 (ρ = +0.791 with |distance|). Region FDR rises with it, 0.547 → 0.924.
- The harness's gap-against-distance ρ here (+0.842) comes mostly from ties at coverage 1.000. **Flag area, not the gap, is the curve to read in this direction**, as T2.4 first found on MRI.
- **Group-conditional calibration** holds 0.873–0.917 with smaller areas everywhere.

**One harness finding for Track 1: where the scales are frozen changes the fix's efficiency.** At 180 views, group-conditional calibration flags **86%** of regions when the combined score's scales are frozen at 30 views (this table), against **12%** when they are frozen at 180 views (§2). The coverage is the same (0.897 and 0.888). D26/D27 freeze the scales at the calibration operator so as not to shrink the gap being measured. The cost shows up in the remote operators' efficiency.

## 3. MRI, the same harness

| | Naive gap-vs-distance ρ | Group-conditional ρ | Naive region FDR | Group-conditional FDR | Group-conditional flag area |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| calibrated at `random1d R2` | +0.316 | +0.105 | 0.000–0.473 | 0.043–0.473 | 0.010–0.556 |
| calibrated at `random1d R8` | +0.556 | +0.158 | 0.035–0.921 | 0.035–0.407 | 0.011–0.544 |

On MRI the signed distance orders naive transfer's gap only weakly. T2.5 §2 and T2.7 §3 showed why: MRI's worst cells (`equispaced1d R2`, and `variable_density2d R2` under the earlier label) sit at **matched acceleration across mask families**, where `null_space_growth` is ~0 because a mask's null-space *size* barely changes. The operators differ in *which* frequencies they drop, which a size-based scalar does not see. On CT the operators differ mainly in how much they measure, so the same scalar does see it.

## 5. Honest limitations

- **Not reportable.** The face prior on both modalities (D-016).
- **One split for the harness tables.** The 13-split robustness of the coverage numbers is in T2.7 §2–3. The harness's flag areas and FDRs here are this split's.
- **Region FDR depends on the positive rate,** which the absolute label makes vary by operator (7.6% at 180 views to 39% at 90° on CT). FDRs are comparable *across methods at one operator*, not across operators.
- **The MRI curve is weak because the distance is scalar.** It summarises null-space size, and MRI's failures are driven by *which* frequencies change. Whether a normalisation makes the two curves collapse is Track 1's test.
- **Distances rest on 32 Hutchinson probes,** with ~1% estimator spread (T2.7 §6).
