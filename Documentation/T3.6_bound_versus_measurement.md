# T3.6 — Bound-Versus-Measurement Study

**Task:** T3.6 — Bound-versus-measurement study
**Track:** 3 — Analytical Foundations and Numerical Verification
**Deliverable (blueprint §18):** Comparison of predicted and observed coverage gaps using the cached score banks
**Status:** Pipeline delivered and run on harness banks in both modalities. **Not run on a real bank**: Track 2's fastMRI and CT banks are local to them (NYU agreement, `banks/README.md`), and the one committed bank has 3 items and no design side. On the harness banks the Step 1 chain `gap ≤ d_K⁺` held in **55 of 57** evaluable ordered pairs, with no pair where the two bootstrap intervals separate; the unconditional bound failed in **10 of 57**, six of them on the CT-like prior bank; the `√d_op` rate bound held everywhere and is **vacuous** everywhere (smallest value 2.06 against a gap that cannot exceed 1). Nothing here is a result about the project.
**Branch:** `T3.6`

---

## Objective

T3.3 derives `gap ≤ d_K⁺` (§2b: Kolmogorov distance of the score laws *given `label = 1`*, with image-level versions) and, under a Lipschitz and a margin assumption, `d_K⁺ ≤ C √d_op / π` (§3b). T3.5 checked the pieces on synthetic operators. T3.6 asks the question the blueprint set: **does the bound sit above the gap that is measured on a score bank, and by how much?** — per ordered operator pair, on the label the project reports (D-061), with intervals over patients, and with every constant of the rate bound estimated from the bank rather than assumed.

**Code.** `src/caliber/theory/bank_bound.py` (`pair_report`), `src/caliber/theory/synthetic_bank.py` (a real-schema bank from the T3.5 harness), `scripts/t3_6_bound_vs_measurement.py` (CLI), `tests/test_bank_bound.py` (9 gates, one of which executes the script). `scripts/t3_5_*.py` now default to the absolute label.

## 2. Method

For an ordered pair `(src, dst)` of a crossed bank (every operator sees every item; `is_crossed`, T1.4):

- **Score and label.** T1.1's standardised affine score with scales frozen on `src` (D27), `s_residual` out (D42, equal weights over the live components). The label is `label_spec`'s: D-061's absolute `err_free > ε`, `ε` the 0.75 quantile of `err_free` on the bank's **design** split pooled over operators and then frozen (`--label-quantile` varies it). Banks without a design side are refused with the hint the repo already gives (`--label ratio` reproduces the superseded label).
- **Measurement.** Fit `split_threshold` on `src`'s `calib`-split positives, read coverage on `dst`'s `test`-split positives (patient-disjoint, D-022). `gap = (1 − α) − coverage_dst`, `α = 0.10`; positive = under-coverage. 90% bootstrap over patients (the item, where the bank has no patient column; D-027).
- **Bound.** Over every non-design row of both operators: `d_K⁺` (Kolmogorov distance of the laws given label 1; exact), its patient-level bootstrap interval, the unconditional `d_K` for contrast, and the §3b decomposition — label-flip probability `P_flip`, crossing probability, `sup|G_A − G_B|`, the exact-algebra bound `(sup|G_A − G_B| + |π_A − π_B|)/π_A` — plus the constants of the rate: `L_S`, `L_ε` (mean absolute change of score and of `err_free` over the coupled pairs divided by `d_op`), `M_S`, `M_ε` (KDE densities; `M_ε` at `ε`), and `π`. **The coupling §3b assumes is observed, not modelled**: the bank is crossed, so the same item and region appear at both operators.
- **Distance.** `d_op` = D19 `null_space_projection` / ambient and the signed `null_space_growth` (D47), from the operators in the bank's manifest — no dataset, no GPU.
- **Directions.** Every ordered pair, never symmetrised (D47). A pair counts as *evaluable* when `dst` has held-out positives and `src` can calibrate.

Banks used (all built by `build_bank` through the real `label_spec` / `derive_score_and_label` path, 15% design / 45% calib / 40% test, identical at every operator):

| bank | operators | items | solver | image |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MRI, noise | `random1d` R = 2, 4, 8, 16 | 40 and 60 | white-noise fill | 128² |
| MRI, prior | same | 60 and 80 | structured toy prior | 64² |
| CT, noise | sparse view, 60 / 30 / 20 / 12 views | 60 | white-noise fill | 32² |
| CT, prior | same | 60 | structured toy prior | 32² |

The committed real-prior bank (`banks/w02_solver_smoke`, 3 items, no design side) runs only with `--label ratio`; it gives gaps of −0.07 and −0.10 against `d_K⁺` of 0.15 and 0.64 and tests nothing.

## 4. Findings

### 4.1 The Step 1 chain holds, with a caveat about sample size

`gap ≤ d_K⁺` held in 55 of 57 evaluable pairs. The two exceptions are the same pair (`R8 → R16`) on the structured MRI prior at the two label quantiles: gap 0.353 against `d_K⁺` 0.318 (q = 0.75), and 0.364 against 0.321 (q = 0.50). In both, the gap interval ([0.301, 0.382] and [0.336, 0.393]) overlaps the `d_K⁺` interval ([0.302, 0.355] and [0.311, 0.344]), so by the criterion `significant_violation` no pair counts against the chain (0 of 57). This is the regime T3.3 §2b(c) predicts: with 16–32 test patients the finite-sample image-level penalty is of the size of the excess, and the proposition's guarantee is vacuous below ~50 patients. A real bank has 44 test patients (fastMRI) — at the edge of that regime, not safely inside it.

### 4.2 The unconditional bound is the wrong object — on stronger evidence than T3.5's

`gap ≤ d_K` (unconditional) failed in 10 of 57 pairs: 2 of 12 on MRI noise (q = 0.75), 1 of 6 and 1 of 9 on MRI prior, and **6 of 12 on the CT-like prior bank**, where the label-conditional law is far from the unconditional one (e.g. `v30 → v12`: `d_K` = 0.66, `d_K⁺` = 0.96, gap = 0.89). T3.5 §6.1 made this point with the superseded ratio label (a conditional gap of +0.52 above an unconditional `d_K` of 0.42); under D-061's absolute label the same point holds, with a different mechanism: the label depends on `err_free` against one global `ε`, so conditioning on it selects the high-error regions, and high-error regions of one operator are different regions from those of another.

### 4.3 The `√d_op` rate bound is valid and useless

No pair violates it. It is also never below 2.06 (a gap is at most 1), with a median 20–143× the Kolmogorov distance. The product `4√(2 M_ε L_ε) + 2√(2 M_S L_S)` is of order 3–10, and then it is divided by `π`. This is the honest status of §3b on these banks: the derivation is sound as an *order* statement, and its constants — dominated by densities that are 3–12 at the scale of scores — are too large for a numeric bound. The exact-algebra stage of §3b (`d_K⁺ ≤ (sup|G_A − G_B| + |π_A − π_B|)/π_A`) is informative only at small shifts (e.g. 0.85 against a measured 0.13 at `R3`, T3.5 §9); at larger shifts it exceeds 1.

### 4.4 A single global ε makes the positive rate operator-dependent

D-061 sets `ε` once per substrate from the design split. On these banks the same `ε` gives `π` of 0.003 to 0.95 across operators (MRI prior, q = 0.50: 0.003 at `R2`, 0.953 at `R16`; CT prior: 0.027 at 60 views, 0.683 at 12). Consequences, each visible in the tables:

- a **source with `π ≈ 0`** has too few positives to calibrate (the repo's `min_positives(0.1) = 9` is not met, `split_threshold` returns −∞, everything is flagged) and its rate bound is divided by `π`;
- a **destination with no held-out positives** has no coverage to measure — and a source with no positives makes the Kolmogorov distance undefined. Excluded pairs per run: 0, 0, 6, 3, 6, 0 in the table's order (MRI-prior and CT-noise banks); they are left out of the `valid` count above, not scored as zero;
- the rows with `gap = +0.89` are thin-source pairs: a threshold fitted on a few high-error positives sits above nearly every destination positive.

This is D3's thin-group warning again, now in the *label* rather than the grouping. It is a consequence of D-061, not of this study; it means the bound's `1/π` factor and the measured gap move together, and that a real bank's per-operator positive counts must be printed beside every coverage number (they are in the CSV: `n_pos_src`, `n_pos_dst`, `pi_src`).

### 4.5 Gap against signed growth

On the CT-like prior bank the gap rises with signed growth at Spearman +0.90 (all six positive-growth pairs under-cover, none of the six negative-growth pairs does). The other runs give +0.10, +0.24, −0.27, −0.52, +0.39 on 6–12 evaluable pairs with confounded `π`. Read against Track 2's real banks (CT +0.976, MRI +0.316, T2.8) this is the same qualitative split — a graded CT curve and a noisy MRI one — and is **not** a replication: the operators, solver, images and sample sizes differ, and the harness MRI family is one-dimensional (`random1d` only).

### 4.6 The over-coverage direction

In 43 of the 72 pairs the gap is −0.100, i.e. coverage 1.00: the source threshold flags essentially everything at the destination. The bound `gap ≤ d_K⁺` holds there trivially (a negative number below a positive one) and says nothing about the real cost, which is flag area. T3.3 §4b already states this; T3.6 measures how common it is — over half of the ordered pairs.

## 5. What this hands upstream

**To T3.3.** (a) §2b stands, with the evidence in §4.1–4.2; the unconditional version should be stated only as the weaker, sometimes-false one. (b) §3b's numeric rate is vacuous on these banks; state it as an order result and present the exact-algebra bound and `d_K⁺` itself as the quantities to report. (c) The `1/π` factor and D-061's global `ε` interact (§4.4); say so where the bound is stated.

**To Track 1 (D-061).** A single frozen `ε` gives operator-dependent positive rates down to 0.003; T1.4's reporting rule should print `n_pos` per operator and treat sources below `min_positives` as uncalibratable, not as a coverage of −0.10.

**To Track 2.** One command produces the table on a real bank (§7). The bank needs a design side, a `patient_id` column (`scripts/add_patient_ids.py`) and, for the CT bank, whatever manifest `rebuild_operators` can read. Reporting is gated on the prior (D-016).

**To T3.7.** The Step 1 chain and its image-level propositions are the derivation to write up; the numeric rate bound is not a headline. The honest sentence is: *the coverage gap never exceeded the Kolmogorov distance between the label-conditional score laws, to within sampling error, on 57 harness pairs; the operator-distance bound on that distance is valid and far from tight.*

## 6. Honest limitations

- **No real bank.** The harness is a toy (white-noise fill, or a blur-prior alternating projection), on phantom images, with one mask family on MRI and a 32² CT operator. Constants and shapes do not transfer.
- **16–32 test patients per bank** (24 in most runs). The bootstrap intervals are as wide as that implies, and §2b(c)'s guarantee is vacuous at that size.
- **Held-out positives are scarce at some operators** (0–5 in several MRI-prior and CT-noise cells), which is why 15 of the 72 pairs are not evaluable; they are excluded, not scored as zero.
- **KDE constants.** `M_S`, `M_ε` depend on the bandwidth (T3.5 §3); they were computed once per pair with a Silverman-type bandwidth and a 20,000-point subsample.
- **`d_op` for CT** is a Hutchinson estimate (32 probes) at 32².
- **The label uses ground truth** and a quantile of `err_free` over operators, both unavailable at deployment.
- **Runs used few bootstrap replicates (60–100)**; interval endpoints move in the second decimal.
